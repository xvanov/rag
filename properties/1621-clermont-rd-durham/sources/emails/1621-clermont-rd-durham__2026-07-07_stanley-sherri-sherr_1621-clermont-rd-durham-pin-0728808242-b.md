Good afternoon Kalin,

Ryan Channell forwarded your email to the Solid Waste Section as the site you referenced is not included in the PRLF program.

I am attaching available records related to the site.

The property is recorded as an open dump and notice is provided to perspective owners that the site has been used a landfill.  To remove the Notice of Open Dump, waste would have to be removed, and soils and groundwater tested to ensure there is no contamination above regulatory limits.  It is suggested that you retain the services of a consultant.  The NCDEQ has a list of Recognized Environmental Consultants available here:  https://www.deq.nc.gov/waste-management/dwm/sf/ihs/rec-program/approved-recs-july-2025-pdf/download?attachment. Contacting one of these firms is probably a good starting point.  They can also suggest relevant soil and groundwater sampling.

It is unlikely that you would be able to build on a landfill waste mass as stability issues and landfill gas generation would be of concern.  You could consult a home builder or engineer regarding these concerns.

You mentioned building in an area of the parcel that does not contain waste.   In that case, you would also need to seek a consultant to help guide you on verifying the waste boundary, in determining if contamination (soil, groundwater, and/or surface water) or landfill gas issues exist on the site that might affect residents of any newly built homes and in buffering or remediating as necessary.  The waste mass would have to be avoided during construction to maintain integrity of cap.

Please let me know if you need additional information.
