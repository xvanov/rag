## Leverage points (why it's worth far less than $140K)
1. **The buyer must BUILD THE ROAD.** ~400 ft of NCDOT-standard road (+ well + septic) — **~$40–100K+** that *every* buyer faces and the seller cannot make disappear. This is a direct, documented value-killer. Frame every dollar of road/utility cost as a dollar off the land.
2. **Thin/again buyer pool.** Most adjacent lots are federal (Corps) — unbuildable/unsalable; this isn't a hot commodity, and a 17-yr vacant hold + the sister-lot price drop signal a **motivated, illiquid seller.**
3. **Unconfirmed buildability = risk discount.** Perc results not yet in hand; road cross-section unconfirmed. A rational buyer prices in that risk (or makes the offer contingent — see below).
4. **Low basis = room to deal.** At ~$16K in, the seller can accept well under $140K and still profit handsomely.

## Tactics
- **Anchor low, justified by cost — not insult.** Open around **$45–60K**, explicitly itemized: "finished-home value − land − ~$Xroad − well − septic − build − margin leaves this." Make the road cost do the arguing.
- **Get the civil road quote FIRST, then use it as an explicit offset.** "Your lot needs ~$Y of road/septic to build; that comes off the price." Facts, not haggling.
- **Make the offer CONTINGENT (free risk transfer):** contingent on (a) acceptable soil/perc results, (b) confirmed road cross-section + cost, (c) legal access/permit path. Walk if any fail — costs you nothing, caps your downside.
- **Negotiate as a cost-driven buyer, not an eager homeowner.** Don't lead with "this is my dream homesite" — emotional buyers pay more. Lead with the build costs.
- **Use time + the price drop.** 17-yr hold, long DOM, and the −$25K drop on the sister lot = they want out. Patience is your friend; there's no competing demand.
- **Assemblage angle (optional):** both parcels are the same owner. A pair deal (1.84 ac) *could* fetch a per-acre discount — but note recombining triggers watershed impervious limits, and you only need one homesite, so don't overpay for the second.

## Target
- **Rational max land bid: ~$40–90K** *contingent on* perc + road cost (per residual, PROPOSAL). 
- **Open ~$50K; walk above ~$90–100K** unless soil + road both come back cheap.
- **Never pay $140K** for a lot that isn't confirmed buildable.
