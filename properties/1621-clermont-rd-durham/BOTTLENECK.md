## Primary bottleneck
**Profitability is undetermined because the all-in COST to make the small SW nub
buildable — a private ROAD build-out on the unmaintained Clermont ROW + a WATER
solution + a mandatory full-depth ENGINEERED FOUNDATION on the disturbed substrate —
is unquantified.** These are the largest costs and the go/no-go decider. We cannot
price an offer until we have a cost range.

The tightest knot is still the **water fork: is a private well permittable on this
former-landfill parcel?** Well (cheap) vs. city-main extension (expensive) is the
single biggest budget swing. The engineered-foundation requirement is now a confirmed
(bounded) cost line, and the access requirement (driveway vs. built road) is the other
big fork.

**What it takes to CLEAR it:** a civil/geotech **feasibility quote** (road + water +
engineered foundation) — which needs two agency answers as inputs: **Weist** (is the
road build-out required?) and **Env Health** (is a well permittable?). Critical path:
`Weist + Env Health answers -> Summit feasibility quote -> cost range -> go/no-go`.

*(NOT the bottleneck — resolved this cycle: zoning [RS-10 OK]; taxes/liens [clean, T-19];
floodway geometry [nub outside floodway, T-20]; **building permit on the landfill** [AHJ
