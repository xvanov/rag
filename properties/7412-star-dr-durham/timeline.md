**T-08 — 2026-07-08 — docrag: second-home whole-house STR is NOT clean by-right.**
If owner lives in home #1 and rents home #2 (separate lot, NOT owner-occupied) entirely to transient
nightly guests: that use is NOT a home occupation (UDO 5.4.4 requires the activity be conducted by
inhabitants residing there). Unlisted STR use gets assigned to the most-similar listed use by the
Planning Director (UDO 5.2.1); the closest listed overnight category is **Hotels/Motels/Extended
Stay Residences** (UDO 5.3), which carries **minor-SUP triggers** when access is through/adjacent to
a residential district/use or the property line is within 200 ft of single-family residential — which
applies here. Building-code-wise a non-owner transient whole-house is R-1 (NC BC 310.2). **So home #2
as a non-owner whole-house STR is the HARDER, discretionary path.** Cleaner alternatives for home #2:
(a) **mid-term 30+ day furnished lease** = ordinary residential tenancy, NOT transient → dodges the
STR classification AND NC occupancy tax; or (b) long-term rental; or (c) spec-build-and-sell.
Owner-occupied ROOM rental in home #1 remains the friendly homestay/B&B path (owner-occupancy met).
Source: Durham UDO §5.2.1 / §5.3 / §5.4.4; NC 2024 BC §310.2/§310.4.2 (docrag).
