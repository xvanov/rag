## Decision-relevant facts extracted
- **Stream = Northeast Creek**; its meandering centerline IS the eastern boundary →
  explains the County's "boundary shifts with creek movement" note. [T-18]
- **North/west neighbor = Parkwood Subdivision, Section 3 (Plat Book 34, Page 16).**
- **South / SW neighbor = "School Property"** = the Durham Public Schools boundary the
  County flagged as hard to track. [T-18]
- Frontage/access: the **Clermont Road ROW eastern terminus is ~257.70 ft east of Euclid
  Road** — consistent with the unmaintained-ROW / build-the-road problem. [T-16]
- Deeded area **9.40 acres** (GIS shows 9.67 — creek-driven discrepancy).
