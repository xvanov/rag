## Where it is
SW Durham County (unincorporated), off Farrington/Stagecoach Rd near **NC-751 / Jordan Lake +
Jordan Game Land**; ~drive to Streets at Southpoint + the RTP / NC-54/55 job corridor. Rural/exurban,
car-dependent (Walk Score 3/100). Jordan Lake recreation is the distinctive local amenity.

## Drivers
**Structural tailwinds (high confidence):**
- **RTP / Research Triangle job + population growth** — Durham County pop **+27% since 2010**; south
  Durham physically wraps RTP. The regional demand engine.
- **Committed road infrastructure (FUNDED / built):** Complete 540 loop **Phase 2 under construction,
  target 2028**; **I-40 widening** (I-85→US-15/501) done ~summer 2026; East End Connector/I-885 opened
  2022; **RDU Vision 2040** new runway under construction + terminal expansion. Improves Triangle-wide
  access → long-run land demand.
- **Jordan Lake** — permanent recreation/open-space anchor; supports STR/second-home appeal but also
  drives the **watershed protection** overlay that constrains development (double-edged).

**Land-use policy (high confidence):**
- **2023 Durham Comprehensive Plan** (adopted Oct 2023) replaced Development Tiers with a **Place Type
  Map + a single Urban Growth Boundary (UGB)**. Broad **27713 sits INSIDE the UGB / urban-services
  area** and is designated for *densifying* suburban/mixed-use along the NC-54/55/Southpoint spine
  (Transit Opportunity Areas, Apartment/Townhouse + Mixed-Use nodes). **SCAD** (eff. Jan 2024) is a
  pro-density code change (no parking minimums, more housing types by-right, smaller lots, ADUs).
  Full UDO rewrite is **on hold (litigation)** — governing code = existing UDO + SCAD.
- **NUANCE for THIS parcel:** it sits at the **far-SW rural/watershed EDGE** of that densifying ZIP
  (unincorporated, well/septic, Jordan Lake watershed) — the ZIP-wide densification story does NOT
  lift this lot's density (watershed + Rural Tier cap it). Parcel-level Place Type returned no polygon
  → confirm on Durham's interactive map.
- **OSBM projections:** Durham Co. 344K (2025) → 375K (2040) → **397K (2050)** — growth *decelerates*
  from ~1.9%/yr historical to ~0.55%/yr. Underwrite the slower official curve, not the 2020–22 peak.

**Speculative / NOT funded (don't underwrite):**
- **Light rail (DOLRT): CANCELLED (2019)** — no successor. **Commuter rail: study-only, FTA declined
  funding (2023)**; region pivoted to **BRT (planning only)**. Real long-tail optionality (a defined
  RTP/south-Durham rail corridor exists on paper) but nothing committed near-term.
- **NC-54 corridor** improvements planned but **construction unfunded (~2033)**; **no funded NC-55
  widening in south Durham** (active NC-55 work is in Apex/Wake).

## Scenarios
- **Base (most likely):** steady exurban appreciation tracking RTP; area stays rural-residential/
  low-density (watershed + Rural Tier zoning cap density). 10-yr: modest real appreciation; 20–30-yr:
  gradual suburbanization pressure from the south-Durham/540 corridor.
- **Bull:** commuter rail/BRT + NC-54 actually funded, RTP boom accelerates, Jordan Lake amenity
  premium → faster appreciation, upzoning pressure. Low probability near-term (transit unfunded).
- **Bear:** watershed/environmental limits + rural zoning freeze development; rates stay high;
  STR regulation tightens → flat real values, thin liquidity for a rural STR-oriented build.
