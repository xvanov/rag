This property lies on an unmaintained right-of-way (pink dots in below image). In order to obtain a building permit, the property owner would need to extend the water/sewer main and build a road to this property. Do note that extending the water/sewer mains and road would be a private endeavor and all costs would be out-of-pocket to the property owner. You will need to consult with a private licensed civil engineer for cost estimates and timeframes.

Sewer (green line) is available through the easement that runs on the property, but the property owner will need to figure out the water and street situation first.

For questions regarding development, please reach out to planning@durhamnc.gov.
