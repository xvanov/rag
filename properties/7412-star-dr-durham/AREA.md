right at the Durham/Chatham + Chapel Hill edge (neighborhood code "I40/751/FATVL/COUNTY LINE").

## Land-use policy (inherited; high confidence)
- **2023 Durham Comprehensive Plan** — Place Type Map + single Urban Growth Boundary; broad 27713
  is inside the UGB and designated for densifying suburban/mixed-use along the NC-54/55/Southpoint
  spine. **SCAD** (Jan 2024) is pro-density (no parking minimums, more housing types, ADUs). Full
  UDO rewrite on hold (litigation) → governing code = existing UDO + SCAD. [212-pine T-05]
- **NUANCE for THIS pocket:** it sits at the far-SW **rural/watershed EDGE** of that densifying ZIP
  (unincorporated, well/septic, Jordan Lake watershed) — the ZIP-wide densification story does NOT
  lift these lots' density; watershed + Rural-Tier zoning cap it. [212-pine T-05]
- **OSBM:** Durham Co. 344K (2025) → 375K (2040) → 397K (2050); growth decelerates to ~0.55%/yr.
  Underwrite the slower official curve. [212-pine T-05]

## Scenarios
- **Base (likely):** steady exurban appreciation tracking RTP; pocket stays rural-residential/
  low-density (watershed + Rural Tier cap). 10-yr modest real appreciation; 20–30-yr gradual
  suburbanization pressure from the south-Durham/540 corridor.
- **Bull:** commuter rail/BRT + NC-54 funded, RTP boom, Jordan Lake amenity premium → faster
  appreciation, upzoning pressure. Low near-term probability (transit unfunded).
- **Bear:** watershed/environmental limits + rural zoning freeze development; rates stay high; STR
  regulation tightens → flat real values, thin liquidity.
