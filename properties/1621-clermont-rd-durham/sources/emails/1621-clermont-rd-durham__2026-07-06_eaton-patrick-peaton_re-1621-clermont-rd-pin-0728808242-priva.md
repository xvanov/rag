Good morning,

Thank you for your inquiry, answers are provided below.

1. Can a private well be permitted on this parcel if sited on the clean SW upland nub, away from the waste footprint?

Unknown.  An application for well permit, site plan, and site visit are required to determine if a permit may be issued.  

2. What water-quality testing, separation distances/setbacks from the former landfill, or construction standards would apply?

Standard testing included with all new well permits tests for coliform bacteria, inorganic analytes, and nitrate/nitrite.  The most restrictive setback is 500 feet from the limit of the landfill to any proposed well site.  The complete NC well construction standards (15A NCAC 02c) are available for download at the following link:
https://www.dph.ncdhhs.gov/programs/environmental-health/site-water-protection-branch/private-wells-program/private-wells-program-rules-and-laws

3. Is there a well/site determination or evaluation process I should initiate now, and what does it require?

An application for well permit is attached.  The application must be signed by the property owner or the owner's legal representative. The application fee is $425.00 and is non-refundable.

Application must include a site plan showing the following:
-proposed building site(s), driveways, utilities, and appurtenances,
-limit of the landfill with setback to the proposed well site,
-Any applicable wetland/stream buffers and no-build lines enforced by the Planning Department.

Please let me know if you have any additional questions.
